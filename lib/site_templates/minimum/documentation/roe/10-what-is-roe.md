# What is Roe?

Roe is an application that gives you the power to:

- create websites in a fun and powerful way
- get a site going in a few days, not weeks or months
- make money through memberships, donations and an online store
- publish writing, podcasts, music and videos
- stay independent, so you are not subject to the whims of tech companies, algorithms and increasing costs.

## Why Should you Use Roe?

It's really fun to use. Most site builders, like Wordpress, Drupal and Substack, are complex, expensive, bloated or they lock you into rigid and restrictive architectures. Roe doesn’t. Instead, Roe uses an extremely simple text formatting language with a few easy-to-learn exentions that allows you to create and organise your content into a light, robust website that you have complete control over.

## How Roe works

Roe builds your website from simple files instead of storing everything in a database.

Most of the time, you'll work with three kinds of files:

- **Markdown files** hold your writing. Markdown lets you add headings, links, and images with ordinary characters, so you never need a complicated formatting tool.
- **Media files** hold the images, audio, and video you add to your site.
- **Settings files** are small files that tell Roe how your site should look and behave.

You don't need to know programming or write a line of code to build a complete website with Roe.

If you already know Markdown, you'll feel at home right away. If you don't, you can learn as you go. The easiest way is the one you just tried: create a post, make a change, and watch the live preview update. If you'd like a reference, keep a [cheat sheet](https://www.markdowntools.io/cheat-sheet) nearby.

## How your site is organized

Most Roe sites have three main parts.

### Header

The header holds your site's navigation. To edit it, open [Admin → Layouts](/admin/layouts).

### Content

This is where most of your work happens. Use the Admin to create and edit:

- Posts — [Admin → Posts](/admin/posts)
- Pages — [Admin → Pages](/admin/pages)
- Products — [Admin → Products](/admin/products) (after you enable the [Store](/documentation/roe/store))

### Footer

The footer holds navigation, copyright, and other site-wide content. To edit it, open [Admin → Layouts](/admin/layouts).

## Building more advanced pages

Once you're comfortable creating posts and pages, Roe can help you build richer layouts — still without writing code. It gives you [Roe-anji](/documentation/roe/roeanji): small additions to Markdown that you combine to make more complex pages.

### Collections

A collection shows a list of your content automatically. You could show your latest posts, featured products, related articles, or documentation pages.

You can place a collection almost anywhere — inside a page, a post, or a product page. [Learn more about Collections →](/documentation/roe/collections)

### Cards

A card inserts a ready-made content block into a page, such as a pull quote, a highlighted note, or a link to another post. [Learn more about Cards →](cards.md)

### Image galleries

An image gallery arranges several images into a tidy layout from only a few lines of Markdown. [Learn more about Galleries →](/documentation/roe/galleries)

## What else can Roe do?

Roe can power much more than a simple website. It also supports:

- blogs and newsletters
- an online store
- paid memberships
- podcasts
- online payments, through Stripe, Snipcart, and Postmark

You can start with a simple website and add these features later, as your needs grow.
