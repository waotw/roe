# How to add your Site Settings

Your Site Settings hold the basics about your site — its name, its address, and who's behind it. You set them in the Admin; there are no files to edit.

1. Open Roe's [Admin](/admin).
2. Select `Settings`.
3. Fill in your site's details:
    - **Site Title** — the name of your site.
    - **Site URL** — your site's web address, for example `https://example.com`.
    - **Site Description** — a sentence or two describing your site.
    - **Author Name** — your name, or your site's default author.
    - **Author Email** — a contact address for your site.
4. Click `SAVE`.

See [Site Settings](/documentation/roe/global-site) for a description of all the Site Settings and what they do.
