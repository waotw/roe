# Getting started with Roe

Welcome to Roe.

If you've already [installed Roe](/documentation/guide-installation), you're ready to build your first page. If you haven't yet, complete [Install Roe](/documentation/guide-installation) first.

## Learn Roe with simple tutorials

The [Docs](/documentation) page has a list of introductory tutorials that flow one in to the next. Go through each one, they won't take longer than a few minutes each. You'll learn some of the most important features of Roe.

## Diving deeper

When you've created a few pages, posts and collections, read this article: [What is Roe?](/documentation/what-is-roe) and visit the full [documentation](/documentation-all) to dive deeper. 

## Let's contribute to a better internet

Roe is a labor of love and it was built for people that makes things. It's a powerful tool and will help you take control over what you publish.

## Need help?

If you run into a problem, have an idea for a new feature, or just want to say hello, we'd love to hear from you. Reach out with [issues](mailto:roe@weareontheweb.com?subject=Roe%20bugs), [feedback](mailto:roe@weareontheweb.com?subject=Roe%20feedback), [feature requests](mailto:roe@weareontheweb.com?subject=Roe%20requests), or a [hello](mailto:roe@weareontheweb.com?subject=Just%20saying%20hello).
